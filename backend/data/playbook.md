# Northwind Labs Contracting Playbook

These are our standard positions. When reviewing a contract, propose edits that bring it in line with them.

## Limitation of Liability

- Cap each party's total liability at **two times (2x) the fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months**.
- Fallback if the other side pushes back: one times (1x) fees.
- Never accept unlimited liability.

## Governing Law

- Governing law must be the **State of Delaware**.

## Payment Terms

- Payment due **Net 30**: within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

## Termination for Convenience

- Require at least **sixty (60) days'** prior written notice.

## Confidentiality (NDAs)

- Confidentiality obligations last **three (3) years** from each disclosure.

## Indemnification

- Indemnities must be **mutual**: each party covers claims caused by its own breach, negligence, or IP infringement.
- Standard carve-outs: no indemnity for claims caused by the other party's own misconduct.
