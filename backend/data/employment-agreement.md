# Employment Agreement

This Employment Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of August 1, 2026 between **Northwind Labs, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the **"Company"**), and **Jordan Rivera** (the **"Employee"**).

## 1. Position and Duties

1.1 The Company employs the Employee as Senior Data Engineer, reporting to the VP of Engineering. The Employee shall devote full working time to the Company's business.

1.2 The Employee confirms that they are not bound by any agreement with a company or other employer that would prevent them from performing these duties.

## 2. Compensation

2.1 The Company shall pay the Employee a base salary of $185,000 per year, paid in line with the Company's normal payroll schedule.

2.2 The Employee is eligible for an annual bonus of up to fifteen percent (15%) of base salary, based on performance goals set by the Company.

## 3. Benefits

3.1 The Employee may take part in the benefit plans the Company offers to similarly situated employees, under the terms of those plans.

## 4. Confidentiality and Inventions

4.1 The Employee shall keep the Company's confidential information secret during and after employment and shall sign the Company's standard invention assignment agreement.

## 5. Non-Competition

5.1 During employment and for twelve (12) months after it ends, the Employee shall not work for or advise a company that competes with the Company in the market for data analytics software in the United States.

5.2 This Section 5 does not stop the Employee from owning less than two percent (2%) of the shares of a publicly traded company.

## 6. Non-Solicitation

6.1 For twelve (12) months after employment ends, the Employee shall not solicit any employee or customer of the Company to leave the Company.

## 7. Termination

7.1 Employment is at will. Either party may end it at any time, with or without cause, on two (2) weeks' written notice.

## 8. General

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and is the entire agreement between the Company and the Employee on its subject matter.
